07 - Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding process, technical stack, and engagement structure.

What is the typical turnaround time for a project? +

For Sprint projects, delivery is completed within 7 business days. Full web builds and multi-agent AI system implementations generally take between 2 to 3 weeks from discovery kick-off to final production launch.

How do you handle ongoing maintenance for AI automations? +

Every project includes automated error reporting telemetry and built-in fallback triggers. All builds come with 30 to 60 days of complimentary post-launch support, with optional monthly maintenance retainers available for continuous updates.

Do I retain full ownership of the codebase and workflows? +

Yes, 100%. Upon final payment, full intellectual property rights, code repositories, automation blueprints, and API credentials are completely transferred to your organization with zero platform lock-in.

What tools and tech stack do you specialize in? +

For web frontend, I write clean, semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and native JavaScript for maximum performance and longevity. For automations, I specialize in Make, n8n, Python, OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude API, and custom vector databases.
